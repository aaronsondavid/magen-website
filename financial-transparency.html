Financial Transparency

Every shekel accounted for.
Every decision defensible.

Magen is funded by the communities we serve. That means we owe those communities complete transparency about how the money comes in, where it goes, and who's watching.

Where the money comes from.

Our funding mix is intentionally diversified. No single donor, foundation, or government line has veto power over Magen's mission.

Private donations

Individual givers of every size, from twenty-shekel Chai gifts to major annual commitments. This is the backbone of the work.

Family foundations

Established relationships with a growing number of Jewish family foundations who fund specific programs or unrestricted operations.

Government grants

Israeli Ministry of Welfare partnership and municipal grants where our mission aligns with public priorities. All grants publicly disclosed.

Crowdfunding

Community-driven campaigns during major initiatives and end-of-year giving. Fully audited and tax-receipted.

Fully accountable.

Four independent structures make sure Magen's finances are handled properly, and that donors can verify it themselves.

US 501(c)(3) via PEF

Fiscal sponsorship through P.E.F. Israel Endowment Funds, Inc., a registered US 501(c)(3). Gifts from US donors are tax-deductible.

Israeli tax status (46A)

Registered Israeli nonprofit with 46A tax-deductibility. Israeli donors receive an official tax-year receipt for every gift.

Independent audit

Annual external audit by a licensed Israeli accounting firm. All statements filed with Israel's Registrar of Non-Profits.

Board oversight

Financial Audit Committee (two board members) reviews finances monthly. Full board reviews performance quarterly.

The governance rhythm.

Financial oversight isn't a once-a-year event. It's a continuous rhythm that keeps decisions defensible in real time.

01

Monthly finance check-in

Board's Financial Audit Committee reviews the month's operations, cashflow, and any variance from budget.

02

Quarterly QBRs

Heads of departments report to the board on program metrics, spending against plan, and strategic decisions.

03

Annual audit

External accountant performs a full audit and issues formal financial statements filed with Israeli regulators.

04

Public reporting

Annual impact report published for donors and the community, alongside downloadable audited financials.
