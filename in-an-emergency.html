In an Emergency

Should the worst happen,
Go to Heder 4.

Heder Arba (חדר 4) is Israel's special emergency unit for survivors of sexual assault. This page walks you through what to do, in three steps. You do not have to do any of it alone.

What is Heder 4?An emergency-room division designed specifically for you.

  • Immediate medical and psychological support
  • Collection of forensic evidence, at your pace
  • Pregnancy and STI prevention
  • Date-rape drug testing
  • Referrals to longer-term support services
01 Before you go

Four things worth knowing on the way. None of them require you to have made a decision yet.

Try not to shower first
Bring the clothes you were wearing during the incident, if possible. Evidence can be preserved best before washing.
Hospitals notify police
But you keep the right to decline filing a formal complaint. Notification is not the same as prosecution.
No immediate decisions needed
Samples are preserved for up to 50 years. You can decide later whether and when to move forward.
Try to arrive within 72 hours
Services are available up to a week after, but the sooner you go, the more options remain open to you.
02 Bring someone with you

You should never have to walk into this alone. Reach out to Magen or your local rape crisis center to arrange a trained volunteer to accompany you through the examination.

Talk to a Magen coordinator, and we'll match you with an accompaniment volunteer, day or night.

Call *9781
