Safety & Healing

The first task of recovery is to establish the survivor's safety. Recovery can only take place within the context of relationships; it cannot occur in isolation. When the truth is finally recognized, survivors can begin their recovery. Judith Herman · Trauma and Recovery

Wherever you are in your safety and healing journey, Magen is here to support you.

01

Muganut

Safety First

Our first priority is to help a victim ensure that they have the physical and emotional safety to begin healing.

We provide guidance if you have concerns about the safety of a child in your life, or if you are confused about what steps to take in your own process.

02

Shikum

Healing is a Journey

The journey to healing is not a linear one, and it can be confusing.

We can help you find the right therapist, navigate the mental health system, and decide if one of our support groups is right for you.

We provide case management to support you, wherever you are in your process.
