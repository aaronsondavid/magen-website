Support Magen

Community. Accountability. Our shared responsibility.

Support survivors, and strengthen the communities we share.

Sexual abuse affects all communities. Equally. The responsibility to prevent it, address it, and respond to it is ours.

What your gift builds.

Three commitments Magen makes to every survivor, every family, and every community we work with. Your gift powers all three.

01 · Active

An active community, where adults take responsibility for children's safety.

Workshops, kallah-teacher training, and educator programs that give parents and community leaders the tools to prevent, recognize, and respond.

02 · Empowered

An empowered community that supports survivors with informed advocacy.

Case management, hotline coverage, legal accompaniment, and therapy referrals so no survivor walks into police stations, courtrooms, or hospitals alone.

03 · Aware

An aware community that understands the process and enables change.

Journalism partnerships, Knesset lobbying, and international coordination so perpetrators can't outrun accountability by crossing a border.
