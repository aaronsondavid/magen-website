Our mission

A lifeline for survivors. A catalyst for change.

Sexual abuse thrives in silence. Magen exists to break that silence, and to make sure no survivor of abuse in the Jewish community has to face what comes next alone.

We do three things, together: we walk with survivors through crisis, healing, and justice; we equip communities to prevent abuse and support the survivors already among them; and we hold perpetrators accountable across borders, working with law enforcement in Israel and the international Jewish diaspora.

The work is rooted in Torah's mandate to protect the vulnerable, and led by evidence-based practice. We don't ask communities to choose between their values and their children. Both are the same commitment.

Community responsibility

This isn't just about your children.
It's about all of our children.

Abuse can't be prevented one household at a time. It's prevented when every adult in a community sees themselves as responsible for every child in that community. When rabbis, teachers, neighbors, and grandparents all know what to look for, how to respond, and where to go for help, silence becomes harder for perpetrators than truth is for survivors.
