Education & Community

Abuse thrives in myth, misinformation, and misunderstanding. Our community-wide approach dismantles taboos and empowers bystanders to recognize red flags and respond.

01

Chinuch

Turning knowledge into protection

We lead workshops, create resources, and train trusted community figures, parents, educators, mikveh attendants, and kallah teachers.

Informed communities and leadership become powerful forces for prevention, healing, and accountability.

02

Kehilla

Systemic change, community-wide

We co-founded Israel's first Knesset lobby and coalition (Shdula) against child sexual abuse.

Lasting change happens when entire communities understand their responsibility: to protect children, support survivors, and create a culture where abuse is neither stigmatized nor silenced.

All of our staff are involved in education, resource creation, and legislative advocacy.

Chinuch & Kehilla in Action

Talking matters, sometimes it's the conversation that saves a life.

Community Workshops

Yaakov attended one of our educational workshops and called the next day to ask for advice. He had five children and had never talked to them about personal safety.

He just didn't know where to start, what words to use, how to bring it up without scaring them. So we spent an hour on the phone walking him through it.

Two hours later, his name flashed on the screen again. This time, he was hysterical. After hanging up, he'd sat down with his 14-year-old son. Before Yaakov could even finish his opening sentence, his son broke down. For two years, their neighbor had been molesting him, two years of silence and fear, until his father's tentative words brought forth a flood.

Yaakov kept repeating the same words through his tears: "You saved my son's life. You saved my son's life."

We didn't save his son. Yaakov did, by showing up, by making that first call, by having the courage to start a conversation he'd been avoiding for years.

Name changed to protect privacy.

FAQ

Our workshops cover age-appropriate body safety, recognizing warning signs, responding to disclosures, creating safe environments, and healing community dynamics.

We emphasize that safeguarding children and supporting survivors are not just compatible with Torah values, they are mandated by them. We prove daily that tradition and child safety aren't competing values; they're inseparable obligations that, when properly aligned, create stronger, safer, and holier communities.

Our approach emphasizes that education creates protection through awareness, not fear. We focus on empowerment rather than frightening statistics, equipping communities with practical tools and understanding. Our educators are trained to discuss difficult topics respectfully, using appropriate language and examples. The goal is a culture of protection and open communication, not anxiety or suspicion.
